# SCOUT.AI — Family Needs & Property Amenity Matrix (`list.md`)

> **Master Persona & Amenity Registry:** Defines relevant questions, amenity priorities, and spatial requirements based on family size and lifestyle needs. Dynamic demands expressed by users are automatically ingested into this matrix to refine interview questions.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Profile Matrix & Relevant Questions

### 1. Nuclear Family with Children (3 – 4 Members)
- **Relevant Chatbot Questions:**
  - *"How many family members will be moving in, and do you need top schools (CBSE/ICSE) or play parks nearby for children?"*
  - *"Do you prefer gated societies with 24/7 security and children's swimming pools?"*
- **Target Amenities & Priorities:**
  - Proximity to top schools (e.g., Greenwood High, Delhi Public School, National Public School).
  - Gated society security, play parks, daycare centers, and pediatric clinics within 2 km.
- **Preferred Layout:** 2 BHK or 3 BHK (1,100 – 1,600 sq.ft.).

---

### 2. Joint / Extended Family with Senior Citizens (5 – 6+ Members)
- **Relevant Chatbot Questions:**
  - *"Will senior citizens be living with you? Do you require ground floor/elevator access and multi-specialty hospitals nearby?"*
  - *"Do you need dedicated 2-car covered parking and a quiet residential layout?"*
- **Target Amenities & Priorities:**
  - Wheelchair/elevator accessibility, ground/lower floor units.
  - Proximity to multi-specialty hospitals (Manipal Hospital, Fortis, Columbia Asia).
  - Quiet green surroundings, pooja room space, and dual car parking.
- **Preferred Layout:** 3 BHK or 4 BHK (1,600 – 2,500+ sq.ft.).

---

### 3. Young Working Professionals & Couples (1 – 2 Members)
- **Relevant Chatbot Questions:**
  - *"Is proximity to Namma Metro stations, tech parks (ITPL, Embassy GolfLinks), and vibrant dining hubs your top priority?"*
  - *"Do you require high-speed fiber internet and power backup for work-from-home?"*
- **Target Amenities & Priorities:**
  - Walking distance (<10 mins) to Namma Metro stations and tech parks.
  - 100% power backup, EV charging points, co-working lounges, and fitness gym.
- **Preferred Layout:** 1 BHK or 2 BHK (600 – 1,200 sq.ft.).

---

### 4. Pet Owners & Active Lifestyle Enthusiasts
- **Relevant Chatbot Questions:**
  - *"Do you have pets or require pet-friendly societies with lake walking trails nearby?"*
- **Target Amenities & Priorities:**
  - Pet-friendly society bylaws, Agara Lake / Ulsoor Lake running tracks, clubhouse sports.
- **Preferred Layout:** 2 BHK or 3 BHK with spacious balconies.
